Common questions

If you don’t see your question here, email us and we’ll reply quickly.

Can someone attend a rental viewing for me in Auckland?
Yes. RentalViewer attends Auckland rental and flat viewings on your behalf. You can join via live video call or receive a recorded walkthrough with honest, independent feedback.
Can I view an Auckland rental property from overseas?
Yes. Many clients are relocating or already overseas. We attend the viewing for you and provide a live call or detailed recorded walkthrough so you can make a decision remotely.
Is RentalViewer affiliated with landlords or agents?
No. RentalViewer is an independent service. We do not work for landlords, property managers, or real estate agents.
What is the difference between a live video call and a recorded walkthrough?
A Live Video Call allows you to join in real time during the viewing and ask questions. A Recorded Walkthrough is filmed during the viewing and delivered to you afterward with written notes.
What do you check during a rental viewing?
We focus on renter-relevant factors including layout and space, natural light, noise levels, street activity, ventilation, visible damp indicators (non-technical), and general condition.
Is this a licensed building inspection?
No. RentalViewer provides a practical, non-technical assessment for renters. If you require a formal building inspection report, you should hire a qualified inspector.
Which Auckland suburbs do you cover?
We cover most Auckland suburbs including Mount Eden, Epsom, Grey Lynn, Ponsonby, Newmarket, Albany, Takapuna, and surrounding areas. Contact us to confirm availability.
How quickly will I receive the video and notes?
Usually the same day as the viewing, depending on schedule. If timing is urgent, mention it when booking and we will confirm.
What do you need from me to book a rental viewing?
Please send the listing link, property address, viewing date and time, and whether you prefer Live Video Call or Recorded Walkthrough. Include any specific concerns you would like checked.
How do payments work?
Bookings are arranged by email. We confirm availability and next steps before the viewing. Payment details are provided during confirmation.
